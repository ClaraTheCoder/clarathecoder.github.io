Introducing Neo, our new approach to creating digital products for the AI era. We’ve always strived to create design systems that enhance customer experiences, drive consistency and help our people work more efficiently. Neo goes further. It reimagines both the experiences our customers see and the way we design and build at scale.

Neo introduces a more expressive digital language. It retains the strongest elements of the Vodafone brand while bringing greater depth, tactility and character through light and shadow, texture and materiality. Familiar at its core, yet unmistakably more modern. From everyday journeys to new propositions and different brands, Neo provides a single foundation from which we can create many different experiences.

But the biggest change is beneath the surface. Neo has been designed for agentic development. Design, engineering and agents now work from the same shared foundation, with components, standards, tokens and technical decisions providing the context and guardrails needed to build consistently across all platforms. That means faster development cycles, less rework and consistently high quality from the outset.

Neo is more than a design system. It is an operating system for digital product development. Built faster, built consistently, built for what comes now and what comes next.

On screen, under the narration: Neo’s components and the screens built from them, on phones, on the web and on a television; a chat with Vodafone’s AI; Design, Engineering and Agents joined to one foundation; Neo’s colour tokens, an architecture decision and code, for the web, Android, iOS and React Native; and Neo drawn as an operating system. It ends on Vodafone’s logo and “Together we can”.
