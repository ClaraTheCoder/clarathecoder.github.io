<!-- Principle post. Notes only. -->

- All-or-nothing adoption asks teams to bet on a system before it has proven itself
- Neo’s three levels: design only → tokens and pipeline → React components
- Tokens are low risk, give immediate brand consistency, and work with any stack
- Neo’s turning point: talking about the token package moved it from a few teams trying it out to widespread adoption
- Each level builds trust for the next, and that needs the architecture to support it from day one (tokens as a separate package)
- Lesson: make the ladder the pitch, not a fallback
- Questions for the post: how to measure adoption at each level, and how to move teams up
